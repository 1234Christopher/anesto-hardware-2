About Us

ANESTO HARDWARE is an indigenous company with world-class services. Our objective is to provide goods and services of international standard, with quality being our watchword.

We at ANESTO are experts in the provision of Industrial, Technical/Oil Tools. Customer satisfaction is paramount to us. We are always ready to promptly deliver quality goods and services to our clients.

Our Mission

  • To provide goods and services that are equal to the task.
  • To reduce man-hour loss by providing the appropriate materials requested.
  • To provide quality goods and services that support our clients' businesses and the growth of the Nigerian economy, while always complying with governing laws, rules, and regulations.
  • To maintain peaceful and mutually beneficial relationships with our clients.

Our Vision

  • To be the leader in providing top-quality Industrial, Technical/Oil Tools.
  • To maintain quality services for our clients at all times.
  • To provide services that meet our clients' demands.
