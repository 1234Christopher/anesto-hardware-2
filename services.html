Hydraulic & pump equipment

We supply Enerpac and Wilden pumps, Wilden repair kits, gauges, hoses, and jacks for compatible hydraulic and fluid-transfer applications.

Share the model, pressure or capacity requirements and intended use so equipment compatibility can be checked.

Torque tools & flow measurement

Torque pumps and hydraulic torque equipment support controlled tightening and loosening of industrial fasteners. Oil flow metres help monitor oil movement in compatible systems.

Contact us with your tool or meter model and operating requirements to check suitable options.

Spill-control materials

Spillage materials, absorbent pads, and booms can help manage leaks and contain or absorb spills in suitable work environments.

Tell us the material involved and where it will be used so the appropriate absorbent type can be considered.

Industrial materials & general contracts

We also support industrial material supply and general contract requirements. For an enquiry, include the requested items, quantities, specifications, project location, and timing so the scope can be reviewed.
